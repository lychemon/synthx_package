# Data

The two panels in this folder are the datasets of Abadie, Diamond, and
Hainmueller (2010, Journal of the American Statistical Association) for
California and of Abadie, Diamond, and Hainmueller (2015, American Journal
of Political Science) for West Germany, as distributed with those papers'
replication materials. They are included to reproduce the results in
Abadie and Shang (2026) and to illustrate the software. Please cite the
original paper when you use either dataset.

listed in the data appendices of the two papers.
